Close console tabs

The Close console tabs step closes all the console tabs in Salesforce console application. When testing a console application, it is recommended to place this step at the beginning of the test to ensure that you are beginning the test with a "clean slate". → To add a Close Console Tabs step:

  1. In the editor, add a step by clicking the + button.

  2. Under the Salesforce steps tab, click Common operations and select Close console tabs step.

  3. When done, click Save to save the test.
