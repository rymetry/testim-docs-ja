Testim integrates with various Test Management Systems. The following integrations are currently supported:

* [TestRail Integration](https://help.testim.io/docs/testrail-integration)
* [qTest Integration](https://help.testim.io/docs/qtest-integration)
* [TTM for Jira Integration](https://help.testim.io/docs/ttm-for-jira-integration)
