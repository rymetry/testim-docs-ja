# Connecting Testim to Trello

Testim creates a new Bug ticket, which includes a detailed description of the bug, including the steps to reproduce the bug, screen resolution and browser, and a screenshot of the bug. To connect Testim to Trello you will have to provide Testim Automate with access permissions as described below.\
:fa-arrow-right: **To Connect Testim to Trello:**

1. Go to **Settings > Bug Tracker**.
2. Click on the **Trello** logo.
3. Click the **Log in** link.

![443](https://files.readme.io/7e41e35-trello1.png "trello1.png")

The following notice is presented.

![673](https://files.readme.io/cea3497-trello2.PNG "trello2.PNG")

4. Click **Log in** and log in to your account.\
   The following notice is presented:

![467](https://files.readme.io/7fdec49-trello3.PNG "trello3.PNG")

5. Click **Allow**.
