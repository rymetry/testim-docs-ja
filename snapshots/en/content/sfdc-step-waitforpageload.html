Wait for Page Load

Salesforce Step This is a Salesforce step.

The Wait for Page Load step waits until the page is fully loaded before moving on to the next step. → To add a Wait for Page Load step:

  1. In the editor, add a step by clicking the + button.

  2. Under the Salesforce steps tab, click Common operations and select Wait for Page Load step.

  3. When finished, click Save.
