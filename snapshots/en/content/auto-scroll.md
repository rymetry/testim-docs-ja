# Auto Scroll

Improve stability by eliminating scroll-related flakiness

By default, Testim will automatically scroll to page elements in your test that are outside the viewport. This increases the stability of your tests and dramatically reduces scroll-related errors.

## Disable Auto Scroll

:fa-arrow-right: **To disable auto-scroll for a test step:**

1. Navigate to **Test List > Tests**.
2. Open a test from the **Test Library**.
3. Click on the test step for which you want to disable auto-scrolling.

![707](https://files.readme.io/29b7146-auto-scroll-step.jpg "auto-scroll-step.jpg")

4. Click **Show Step Properties** in the action menu.

![301](https://files.readme.io/af61ba7-show-step-properties-gear-icon.jpg "show-step-properties-gear-icon.jpg")

5. Select **Disable auto-scroll**.

![256](https://files.readme.io/be07acd-disable-auto-scroll.jpg "disable-auto-scroll.jpg")
