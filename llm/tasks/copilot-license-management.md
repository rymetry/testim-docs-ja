Company owners can manage the Copilot Licenses. As a Company Owner, you can assign or remove users to license seats.

# Viewing current Copilot license allocation

:fa-arrow-right:**To view the current Copilot license allocation:**

1. Click on your user avatar.
2. Under **Company**, click your company name.
3. Click the **Plans** tab.
4. Click the **Copilot** button.

   ![](/images/project-user-management/copilot-license-management/e2220b0-copilotlicense1.png)
5. The number of licenses that are currently assigned out of the total number of available seats is indicated in the Copilot User Licenses card. The graph indicated the total number of assigned seats (green) out of the total number of available seats (gray).
6. Under the **Company Teammates** you can find a list of all the users in the company. Users that have been assigned to Copilot are indicated as "Assigned".

   ![](/images/project-user-management/copilot-license-management/df6a668-copilotlicense2.png)

# Assigning seats to users

To assign a seat to a user:

1. Under Copilot User Licenses, click **Assign Seats**.\
   The **Assign Copilot Seats to Teammates** is displayed:

   ![](/images/project-user-management/copilot-license-management/88be1cb-assign.png)
2. Click one of the users that is not currently assigned.
3. Click **Confirm**.

# Removing an assigned user

:fa-arrow-right:**To remove an assigned user:**

1. Under the **Company Teammates** list, right click on an assigned user.
2. Select **Remove**.

   ![](/images/project-user-management/copilot-license-management/26ec1ee-remove.png)
